Bot or webhook?

Both send the identical embed. The difference is who appears to be talking, and what the message can do afterwards.

 Bot messageWebhook
Sender Your bot's name and avatar, APP tag Anything — Daydream Uploads, or a per-platform name and avatar
Setup Nothing extra Create a webhook on the channel, paste the URL into the account
Auto-thread Yes No
Crosspost to followers Yes No
Rate limits Shared with everything else the bot does Its own bucket — better when five platforms fire at once

Rule of thumb: #uploads as a webhook for the branded sender, #live-now as the bot so it can open a thread. Set webhookUrl on an account to switch; leave it empty to post as the bot.

Who gets pinged

Every specimen above pings a role, never @everyone. One role per platform means someone who only cares about streams isn't woken by a TikTok — and people who are never woken by the wrong thing don't mute the server.
